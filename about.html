OUR STORY

Built on Experience. Evolving for the Future.

BHABHA GLOBAL TAX AND ADVISORY represents more than a new professional-services brand. It represents the continuation of a family legacy in accounting and bookkeeping that began in the early 1980s.

Our journey started with Mr. Khiv Singh Sankhala, who began his professional career in accounting and bookkeeping more than four decades ago. Through years of hands-on experience, professional discipline, and close relationships with clients, he built a practice serving businesses across a wide range of industries.

In 2005, after completing his Postgraduate studies in Commerce in 2004, Sandeep Sankhala joined his father and became the second generation of the family to enter the profession. Working alongside his father, Sandeep helped expand the practice, broaden its client base, and develop experience across diverse business sectors.

Over the years, our professional experience has extended across civil contracting, hotels, restaurants, hospitality services, retail and wholesale trading, petrol pumps, LPG gas distribution, and numerous other businesses.

FROM ONE GENERATION TO THE NEXT

A Legacy That Continues. A Vision That Expands.

The next chapter of our family journey is now taking shape with Bhavya Sankhala, who is pursuing her graduation in Commerce and working towards the ACCA qualification.

Bhavya represents the next generation of our accounting legacy—bringing a contemporary perspective, international professional aspirations, and a strong interest in modern accounting and finance practices.

With the foundation established by her grandfather and strengthened through the work of the second generation, the family is now preparing to build a professional platform designed for the next generation of clients.
